# Domain patterns (read during Setup to tailor the schema)

- **Personal (goals, health, psychology, self-improvement):** sources = journal entries, articles, podcast notes. Entities: habits, people, experiments. Concepts: themes, patterns. Add a `timeline.md`. Be gentle, factual, and private; mark inferences as hypotheses.
- **Research deep-dive:** sources = papers, reports. Maintain an evolving **thesis** in `overview.md`, a `questions.md` of open questions, and comparison tables of methods/results. Track claim strength and date.
- **Book companion:** ingest chapter by chapter. Pages for characters, places, themes, plot threads; a running timeline; avoid spoilers beyond the chapters ingested (record "as of chapter N"). Think fan-wiki style.
- **Business / team:** sources = Slack threads, meeting transcripts, docs, customer calls. Entities: customers, projects, decisions, people. Add decision logs and owner fields; suggest human review of updates.
- **Competitive analysis / due diligence:** entities per company/product; comparison matrices; claims dated and sourced; flag stale data.
- **Trip planning / course notes / hobby:** lightweight; entities = places/lectures/gear; concepts = themes; an itinerary or study-guide analysis page.

Pick the closest pattern, mention the choice in one line, and record domain rules in the schema's "Domain-specific notes".
